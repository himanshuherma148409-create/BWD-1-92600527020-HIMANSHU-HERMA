Streams and Subjects

  1. Science
    1. Physics

      Physics is the study of matter, energy, force, and motion.

    2. Chemistry

      Chemistry deals with substances, their properties, composition, and reactions.

    3. Biology

      Biology is the study of living organisms and life processes.

  2. Commerce
    1. Accountancy

      Accountancy involves recording, classifying, and summarizing financial transactions.

    2. Business Studies

      Business Studies focuses on business management and organizational operations.

    3. Economics

      Economics studies the production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services.

  3. Arts
    1. History

      History is the study of past events and civilizations.

    2. Geography

      Geography studies Earth's physical features, environment, and human activities.

    3. Political Science

      Political Science examines governments, political systems, and public policies.
